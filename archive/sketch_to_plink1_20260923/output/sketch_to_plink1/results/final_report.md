# Sketch-to-synthetic-genotype experiment

**Conclusion: the frozen single-theta rule does not meet the predeclared LD-preservation contract. The separate two-stratum follow-up also fails its gates.** The final decision is **unsupported** for `theta_chr22 -> acceptable LD preservation on held-out chromosomes`; there is no accepted genome-wide conversion.

The experiment produced 10,000 synthetic rows from the ALT-oriented sketch using the pinned R 4.3.3 / pecotmr 0.3.4 / pgenlibr 0.3.2 runtime. PVAR and AFREQ were joined by normalized chromosome, position, and unordered allele set, with exact ID/REF/ALT cross-checks. The inspected sketch generator standardizes ALT dosage, projects it, and applies a positive min-max rescale to [0,2]. Thus ranks and Pearson r are unchanged by the omitted affine inverse, and no extra `2-D` allele flip was applied. The installed PGEN reader returned stored values unchanged with `meanimpute=FALSE`; malformed/nonfinite columns were excluded before scoring. The documented executed tutorial was absent at its expected COSI_paper path, so orientation was resolved from the retained generator and installed reader implementation. The same 10,000 PSAM row-order hash was observed across chromosomes 1, 6, 19, 21, and 22.

The target is Pearson LD among the 10,000 source sketch rows, not independently observed original-genotype LD. Region selection used target/input summaries only: 24 chr22 candidate windows and four candidate windows on each of chr1, chr6, chr19, and chr21 were screened; eight chr22 regions and three regions per held-out chromosome were frozen, with two development regions in each target-LD × rare-content quadrant. The chr6 region at 30.78–30.87 Mb overlaps the MHC interval. Every selected region contains 1,000 eligible variants. The fixed pair universe contains 628,929 pairs across the 20 regions, with 16 nearest and up to 16 seeded later pairs per SNP within 1 Mb. There are 16,157 SNPs and 3,843 indels in the pilot.

The 17-point chr22 grid and eight-point refinement completed across all eight development regions. The frozen parameter is **theta=1.4305555556**. It modestly improves on theta=1: development weighted RMSE is 0.114764 versus 0.116616 for theta=1. All 24 held-out jobs completed at theta* and theta=1. Held-out weighted RMSE is 0.133228 versus 0.134954 for theta=1. The improvement over the HWE baseline is small (0.00173 held out), and neither parameter meets the 0.10 absolute RMSE gate.

The held-out chromosome weighted RMSE values are 0.122918 (chr1), 0.116493 (chr6), 0.120763 (chr19), and 0.166541 (chr21); chr21 fails the 0.15 chromosome gate. Fourteen held-out LD×MAF pair cells met the minimum support of 1,000 pairs across at least three regions. Some supported cells fail badly: strong-LD RMSE is 0.29640 for MAF <0.01 pairs and 0.18694 for 0.01–0.05 pairs, compared with 0.12776 for pairs where both MAFs are >=0.05. The main region-level bootstrap (20,000 resamples, fixed seed 20260923) gives development RMSE 95% interval [0.1099, 0.1198], held-out [0.1141, 0.1573], and held-out-minus-development [-0.00135, 0.04296]. Regions, rather than correlated pairs, were resampled.

The corrected dense-LD audit evaluates all 124,750 unordered pairs among 500 variants in one prespecified development region and one held-out region. Dense signed-r RMSE is 0.07540 (chr22) and 0.05540 (chr1), with vector correlations 0.97424 and 0.96530. These subsets agree with the bounded-pair view; they do not remove the failure in strong-LD, MAF-stratified, chromosome, or absolute-error gates. Pairwise attainable-correlation bounds were computed for 680 fixed sampled pairs from the frozen marginals; see `pairwise_feasibility_sample.tsv`. They do not establish simultaneous multivariate feasibility.

All retained variants had tied stored values; the mean fraction of excess tied rows was 37.42%, and the mean largest tie group was 0.0742% of rows. These are observed input properties and may contribute to quantization behavior; this experiment does not establish ties as the sole cause. Frequency error meets the finite-count bound, but genotype proportions and HWE heterozygosity remain poor in substantial MAF regimes. The complete eligibility/exclusion census is `metadata_exclusion_census.tsv`; all selected regions had zero malformed source columns. Theta=0 was evaluated but globally invalid because one fixed chr22 development region had a candidate-induced constant column. The reducer did not score a partial subset for that theta.

Because held-out pair errors showed a clear MAF effect, the assignment’s two-stratum follow-up was run as a separate versioned experiment. Chr22 screening selected candidates {2.0, 1.875, 1.75} for MAF <0.05 and {1.375, 1.40278, 1.43056} for MAF >=0.05; all nine joint pairs were evaluated on the complete original chr22 fixed-pair universe. The frozen pair is (theta_low=2.0, theta_high=1.4305556). Its chr22 loss is 0.114596. On the six untouched chr2/chr20 regions, weighted RMSE is 0.122353 versus 0.124111 for the (1,1) baseline. Transfer difference is 0.00776 and ratio 1.068, but the absolute development/held-out RMSE gates still fail, strong-pair bias is -0.12838, and only 54.0% of MAF>=0.05 variants meet the heterozygosity tolerance. Twelve held-out LD×MAF cells have adequate pair support; strong-LD RMSE is 0.27013 for <0.01/<0.01 pairs and 0.16179 for 0.01–0.05/0.01–0.05 pairs. The two-stratum result is also **unsupported**.

The pilot PLINK1 dataset is an experimental artifact, not an accepted release: 10,000 synthetic samples × 20,000 variants, BED size 50,000,003 bytes. PLINK 1.9 rewrote the BED byte-identically with `--keep-allele-order`; 32 stratified variants were independently recoded and all 10,000 calls matched the generated ALT genotypes. BIM A1 is ALT, the sidecar records REF/ALT, and the FAM uses synthetic IDs. No original individual genotypes, participant lists, or projection matrix W were read. PGEN full-file hashes were not calculated; file sizes/timestamps and per-region source-subset content hashes are retained.

Execution states are distinct: preparation completed; 136 coarse-grid jobs and 64 refinement jobs completed (the theta=0 candidate was mathematically invalid in one region); parameters were frozen before 24 held-out jobs were created; all held-out jobs completed; the pilot files passed PLINK1 verification; scientific acceptance failed. The main LSF job IDs were 270018535 (pilot), 270019219 (coarse array), 270020605 (refinement), and 270022434 (validation). The follow-up used 270039216 (successful pilot), 270039771 (development), and 270041975 (reserved validation). Native logs and the literal runnable command path are retained under `logs/` and [RUN.md](../../experiments/sketch_to_plink1/RUN.md).

The primary parameter freeze and machine-readable gate results are in `frozen_parameters.json` and `acceptance.json`. Numeric figure sources are listed in [figure_captions.md](figure_captions.md). The main code, supporting audits, pair caches, ranks, result tables, bootstrap intervals, frequency diagnostics, dense audit, pairwise bounds, and PLINK1 round-trip evidence are all under `experiments/sketch_to_plink1/` and this ignored output directory. No genome-wide output was produced because the frozen single-theta acceptance contract failed.
